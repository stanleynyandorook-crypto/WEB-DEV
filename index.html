    About SkyNest

    SkyNest Co-Living Spaces is a Singapore-based co-living company built for
    people who move often — remote workers relocating for a project, students
    starting a new term, professionals between long leases. We handle the
    furniture, the Wi-Fi and the community; you handle the living.

    Shared facilities on site

    Since demand for flexible housing has grown alongside remote work and
    study-abroad relocation, we built SkyNest around one idea: a place should
    be easy to move into and hard to want to leave. Every resident gets a
    private, furnished room and full access to shared spaces designed for
    working, cooking and unwinding together.

    Not sure which stay fits your plans? Our team can put together a custom
    package for corporate bookings, long stays, or specific accessibility needs.
