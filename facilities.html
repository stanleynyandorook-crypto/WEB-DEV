    Everything you need, under one roof

    Every SkyNest room comes with full access to the facilities below — no extra booking required for the essentials.

    Core facilities

    Fully furnished private rooms

    A bed, desk, storage and linens are ready before you arrive — just bring your bags.

    Shared co-working spaces

    Dedicated desks separate from your room, for when you need a proper work setup.

    Communal kitchens & dining

    Full kitchens shared across each floor, plus dining areas for cooking together.

    High-speed Wi-Fi

    Building-wide coverage, included in every stay with no separate router to manage.

    Laundry rooms

    On-site washers and dryers, so laundry day never means leaving the building.

    24/7 security & key-card access

    Controlled entry throughout the building, with security on site around the clock.

    Extra perks

    The details that turn a room into a place you actually enjoy coming back to.

    Key-card access, lifts to every floor, and step-free routes to shared spaces are standard across the building. If you have specific accessibility needs, our team can match you to a suitable room and put together a custom package — just get in touch.
